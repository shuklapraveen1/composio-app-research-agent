Methodology

Four channels, one precedence order

Channel A researches all 100 apps once. B, C and D run only over two seeded samples of 30 apps total, plus any review trigger.

A

Primary research

Classifies every app from a reviewed source corpus — every value traces to a cited URL.

B

Independent re-reading

A second pass with different tie-breaks, over the sampled apps only.

C

Citation audit

Checks whether each cited source actually supports the claim it's attached to.

D

Human adjudication

Resolves disputed fields. Precedence runs D beats C beats B beats A.
